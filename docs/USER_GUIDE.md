# VendorHub User Guide

## 1. Customer App

1. **Register / Login** — create an account or sign in.
2. **Browse** — browse the catalogue by category, or scroll the vertical video
   feed and buy the featured product instantly.
3. **Cart** — adjust quantities; the cart totals update live.
4. **Checkout** — choose a delivery address, payment method and order type
   (delivery / pickup / dine-in).
5. **Track** — follow your order and delivery in real time on the map.
6. **Subscriptions** — subscribe to a plan for perks (feature-flagged).
7. **Orders** — view order history, status and reorder.

## 2. Vendor & POS App

1. **Login** — sign in as a vendor.
2. **Orders** — accept / reject incoming orders and advance them through the
   status flow (confirmed → preparing → ready → out for delivery).
3. **Print** — connect an ESC/POS Bluetooth printer under Printer, then print
   receipts directly from an order.
4. **Products** — create, edit and deactivate products.
5. **Inventory** — adjust stock. Changes are cached offline and synced when
   connectivity returns.
6. **Branches** — manage your branch locations and operating hours.
7. **Earnings** — monitor today / week / month earnings.

## 3. Driver App

1. **Login** — sign in as a driver.
2. **Deliveries** — accept an available delivery. A pickup/in-transit flow
   guides you from the vendor to the customer.
3. **Track** — the driver app records your GPS position in the background and
   uploads it in batches when online.
4. **Proof of delivery** — capture a photo and add notes on completion.
5. **Earnings** — review your daily/weekly/monthly earnings ledger.

All three apps work offline when appropriate; queued changes sync
automatically once the connection is restored.
